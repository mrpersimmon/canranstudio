## 主图提示词

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: close-up keepsake illustration for a children's English stationery shop lesson, 3:2 landscape, genuinely transparent background. Reference images ONLY for rounded dark cocoa outlines, warm paper texture, flat soft colors, large expressive heads and friendly illustrated adults; do NOT copy their people or objects. New identities: on LEFT a lady customer, chin-length dark auburn bob, small oval green hair clip, ivory scarf, muted teal short jacket, terracotta skirt. On RIGHT a male shop assistant, short curly ginger hair, small square spectacles, rolled lavender shirt sleeves, pale golden apron. Face each other. Key event: assistant returns a few plain gold coins into customer's open palm over a stationery counter; customer looks delighted, assistant warm slightly sheepish smile. Their faces, complete hands, eye contact and the coins dominate the middle close-up. On counter lower edge, one neat stack of large cream envelopes, one blue writing pad and one small glue bottle, no chalk purchase. Very light ghosted paper shelves behind them, no large opaque background. Adult characters rendered in charming child-friendly keepsake style, not realistic. Safe 6 percent padding around hair/hands; intentional waist-up composition, no chopped fingers. No words, logos, labels, stars, border, titles, name or date. No banknotes or amount. Distinct silhouettes from the umbrella and handbag references. Usable as shared illustration in a responsive reward card.
