The attached office and coffee illustrations are STYLE references only: same confident dark chocolate-brown rounded contour lines, friendly rounded human faces with small oval eyes, rosy cheeks, soft cream paper/gouache texture, warm matte colors, clear readable silhouettes. Do not copy their characters. Landscape composition approximately 4:3 with two distinctive characters large and close enough to read on a child's small reward card. LEFT: friendly stocky middle-aged butcher, small dark brown moustache, round face, white butcher cap, white shirt with sleeves rolled up, red-and-cream vertically striped apron. RIGHT: Mrs Bird, a graceful middle-aged woman with dark chestnut hair in a tidy high bun, a small ochre hat, sage-green coat with cream collar, warm smile. The butcher faces right toward Mrs Bird; she faces left toward him. Both look at the handoff. They jointly hold one small tan paper parcel tied with red string over the shop counter, conveying an attentive completed purchase. Her other hand holds a small wicker shopping basket. A single butcher's balance scale and a clean display tray containing one stylized steak are on the counter. Simplified red-and-cream striped awning and warm shop shelving only behind them, fading to genuine transparent edges. No text, letters, numbers, logos, ribbons, stars, or card border. No knife, cleaver, gore, animal carcasses, or photoreal meat. All gestures, all fingers, and both heads intact. Compact, polished, charming human story scene, not a generic avatar collage. Preserve alpha outside the composed scene.
