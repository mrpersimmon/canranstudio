## 纪念卡

Create a new transparent-background children's English lesson keepsake illustration. Use the attached image ONLY as an exact STYLE reference: warm storybook cuteness, confident dark cocoa outlines, round clean faces with small black oval eyes, subtle paper/grain shading, polished uncluttered forms. NEW CHARACTERS AND STORY: two friendly adult women having coffee at a small round cafe table, waist-up close-up composition. Christine on LEFT has a sleek dark almost-black rounded bob, a tiny cream hair clip, ochre cardigan with cream collar. Ann on RIGHT has auburn curly short hair, sage green blouse with scalloped cream collar. Both are clearly women, distinct from the reference people. They face and look at one another. Christine smiles gently and offers a single golden biscuit on a small saucer with ONE hand. Ann smiles gratefully, with ONE hand accepting the saucer and the other hand holding a teal ceramic coffee cup. Every hand is correctly attached and no extra limbs. Small sugar bowl on table, an untouched little milk jug farther away; coffee is dark black, not white. A softly simplified arched window and tiny plant behind them, kept as a small vignette, no full room rectangle. Tightly framed on their large expressive faces and the interaction, no large empty space, all outer shapes uncropped with 5% padding. No text, labels, stars, numbers, lettering, logos, frames or badges. Landscape aspect around 1.4:1. Transparent outside the vignette, actual alpha. The result will sit in the right half of a certificate with all text added separately by code.
